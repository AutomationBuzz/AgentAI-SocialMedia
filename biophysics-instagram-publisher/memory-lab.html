Important: This simulation is a visual teaching aid. Its arbitrary index and curve do not represent a biological measurement, individual outcome, or clinical memory test. Sleep and practice can support learning, but neither guarantees retention.

Open questions: Researchers study engrams, sleep-related replay, and how memories change. Many detailed causal findings come from animal work; how neural processes relate to conscious experience remains unresolved.
